# Personal Auto Policy

Policy form AP-2026-01. Issued by Example Mutual Insurance Co. This is a fictional sample document created for software testing.

## Agreement

In return for payment of the premium and subject to all the terms of this policy, we agree with you as follows. Throughout this policy, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the Declarations and the spouse if a resident of the same household. "We", "us" and "our" refer to Example Mutual Insurance Co.

## Definitions

"Covered auto" means any vehicle shown in the Declarations, a newly acquired auto reported to us within 14 days of purchase, and a temporary substitute auto used while your covered auto is out of normal use because of breakdown, repair or loss.

"Collision" means the upset of your covered auto or its impact with another vehicle or object.

"Actual cash value" means the cost to repair or replace the property minus depreciation for age, wear and condition.

## Part A - Liability Coverage

We will pay damages for bodily injury or property damage for which any insured becomes legally responsible because of an auto accident. We will settle or defend, as we consider appropriate, any claim or suit asking for these damages.

### Limits of Liability

The limits shown in the Declarations are the most we will pay for any one accident, regardless of the number of insureds, claims made or vehicles involved. Unless the Declarations show otherwise, the limits are:

- Bodily injury: $100,000 per person and $300,000 per accident.
- Property damage: $50,000 per accident.

### Exclusions for Part A

We do not provide liability coverage for any insured:

- Who intentionally causes bodily injury or property damage.
- While using a vehicle to carry persons or property for a fee, including ridesharing, unless the Rideshare Endorsement (Form AP-RS-12) is attached to this policy.
- While participating in any racing or speed contest.

## Part D - Coverage for Damage to Your Auto

We will pay for direct and accidental loss to your covered auto, minus any applicable deductible shown in the Declarations.

### Deductibles

| Coverage | Deductible |
|---|---|
| Collision | $500 per claim |
| Comprehensive (other than collision) | $250 per claim |
| Glass repair | $0 |
| Glass replacement | $100 |

If two or more of your covered autos are damaged in the same collision, only the highest applicable deductible will apply.

### Rental Reimbursement

If your covered auto cannot be driven because of a covered loss, we will pay up to $40 per day for a rental vehicle, for a maximum of 30 days per loss.

### Exclusions for Part D

We will not pay for loss to your covered auto caused by wear and tear, freezing, mechanical or electrical breakdown, or road damage to tires. This exclusion does not apply if the damage results from the total theft of your covered auto.

## Part E - Duties After an Accident or Loss

We have no duty to provide coverage under this policy unless there has been full compliance with the following duties.

You must report the accident or loss to us within 30 days. Report it by calling the claims line at 1-800-555-0100 or through the online claims portal.

A person seeking coverage must:

- Cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement or defense of any claim or suit.
- Promptly send us copies of any notices or legal papers received in connection with the accident or loss.
- Submit to a physical examination by a physician we select, at our expense, as often as we reasonably require.
- Take reasonable steps after a loss to protect the covered auto from further loss. We will pay reasonable expenses incurred to do this.
- Report a theft of the covered auto to the police promptly.

## Policy Period and Cancellation

This policy applies only to accidents and losses which occur during the policy period shown in the Declarations. You may cancel this policy at any time by notifying us in writing. We may cancel for nonpayment of premium by mailing notice at least 10 days before the cancellation date.
