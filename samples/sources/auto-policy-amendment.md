# Personal Auto Policy Amendment No. 1

Amends policy form AP-2026-01. Issued by Example Mutual Insurance Co. This is a fictional sample document created for software testing.

## Effective Date

This amendment is effective January 1, 2027, and applies to policies issued or renewed on or after that date. It changes the Personal Auto Policy as described below. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Changes to Part D - Coverage for Damage to Your Auto

Collision deductible: the deductible for collision is increased from $500 to $750 per claim.

Rental reimbursement: the daily amount for a rental vehicle is increased from $40 to $50 per day. The maximum of 30 days per loss is unchanged.

## Terms Not Changed

This amendment does not change the liability limits under Part A, the comprehensive deductible, or the glass repair and glass replacement deductibles.
