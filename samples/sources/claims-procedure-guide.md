# Claims Procedure Guide

Guide version CG-2026. Published by Example Mutual Insurance Co. This is a fictional sample document created for software testing.

## How to File a Claim

Step 1 - Make the area safe and prevent further damage. Keep receipts for any emergency repairs.

Step 2 - Report the claim by calling 1-800-555-0100, 24 hours a day, or through the online claims portal. Have your policy number, the date and location of the loss, and a description of what happened ready.

Step 3 - Document the loss with photos or video, and keep damaged items until an adjuster has inspected them.

Step 4 - An adjuster will contact you. We acknowledge every new claim within 10 business days.

Step 5 - Send any documents we request, such as repair estimates, police reports or medical bills.

## Timelines

| Stage | Our standard |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge the claim | Within 10 business days |
| Accept or deny the claim | Within 30 days of receiving all required documents |
| Pay an accepted claim | Within 30 days of acceptance |

If we need more time to investigate, we will tell you in writing why, and give an update at least every 30 days.

## Appeals

If you disagree with a claim decision, you can request a review. Send your appeal in writing within 60 days of the date of the decision letter, and include any new information that supports your claim. An appeal is reviewed by a person who was not involved in the original decision. We will send a written response within 30 days of receiving the appeal.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a claim containing false or misleading information may be committing insurance fraud. Fraudulent claims will be denied and may be reported to the authorities.
